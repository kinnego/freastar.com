Supporting local caterers and local schools

Freastar Meals is designed to help local catering companies deliver a professional lunch ordering service to nearby schools. By giving caterers the tools for online ordering, menu management, allergen visibility, kitchen summaries, labels and reporting, Freastar helps local providers offer the kind of digital experience usually associated with much larger operators.

Local providers

Give local caterers the tools to serve nearby schools professionally.

Fresher, community-focused food

Support a model where food can be prepared closer to the schools and communities it serves.

Less admin, better service

Online ordering, reporting, labels and kitchen summaries help caterers spend less time fighting spreadsheets and more time delivering a reliable service.

Built from real-world school needs

Our systems are designed around practical workflows: ordering, labels, allergens, reporting, absences, invoicing and communication. We focus on solving the admin challenges that schools and caterers face every day.
