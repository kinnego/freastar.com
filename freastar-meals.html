Built for local school catering

Freastar Meals is not a catering company. It is the software platform that helps caterers, schools and parents manage school meals more clearly. The goal is to make it easier for local caterers to provide high-quality lunches to local schools, with the digital tools needed for ordering, allergens, labels, absences, reporting and invoicing.

Order on any device, see exactly what their kids are eating, manage allergies safely, and handle payments seamlessly.
