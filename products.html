Freastar Meals

The software that lets any local caterer participate in Ireland's Hot School Meals Programme

Ireland's Hot School Meals Programme — funded by the Department of Social Protection and now reaching hundreds of thousands of primary pupils — has outgrown spreadsheets and phone calls. Freastar Meals is the end-to-end platform that gives local catering companies everything they need to join the scheme and run it with full transparency: parent ordering, allergen tracking, nutrition visibility, kitchen production, accurate uptake data and audit-ready reporting for DSP claims.

Why it matters now

  • Lets any local caterer meet the operational and reporting demands of the Hot School Meals Programme
  • Produces clean uptake and claim data in response to recent C&AG findings on claim verification
  • Supports menu, portion-size and nutritional-standards tracking as the scheme evolves
  • Reduces waste through accurate daily ordering and absence handling

Key Features

  • Parent ordering and child profiles
  • Allergy tracking and meal rules by school
  • Menu assignment by day with nutritional visibility
  • Kitchen summaries and printable meal labels
  • QR code lookup for meal and allergen details
  • Absence tracking and flexible ordering
  • Invoicing, uptake data and DSP-ready reporting
  • Catering company and school account management
  • Installable PWA that works across devices

SchoolPledge.ie

Helping schools and families act together

SchoolPledge.ie helps schools, parent groups and communities create shared digital pledges around issues that matter to children and families - including smartphone-free childhood initiatives, online safety and community wellbeing.

Designed to make collective action simple, supportive and visible, the platform helps parents feel they are not acting alone.

Key Features

  • Create school or community pledge pages in minutes
  • Encourage collective participation from parents and families
  • Track community support and pledge uptake
  • Share initiatives publicly with simple branded pages
  • Reduce pressure on individual parents through community alignment
  • School-friendly setup designed for parent associations and local groups

Whether your community is encouraging delayed smartphone use, promoting healthier digital habits or supporting a shared wellbeing initiative, SchoolPledge.ie helps communities come together around a common goal.

LiveOnWith.me

A place for their story — a private digital memorial platform

LiveOnWith.me is a gentle, family-controlled space to preserve and share the stories, voices and photographs of someone who has passed away. Built for families and funeral directors who want a dignified alternative to social media, it keeps a loved one's memory alive without public engagement metrics or distracting feeds.

Key Features

  • Curated storytelling of life milestones and cherished moments
  • Shared photograph collection with family approval before publishing
  • Memory contributions from friends, colleagues, neighbours and extended family
  • Cemetery search to locate existing memorials
  • Family moderation of every contribution
  • No like counts or public engagement metrics — made for remembering, gently and privately

Suitable for families creating a lasting memorial, and for funeral directors offering a modern memorial service to the families they care for.
