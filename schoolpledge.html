About SchoolPledge.ie

SchoolPledge.ie is a lightweight pledge system built by Freastar to help schools and parent groups create digital pledge pages for community initiatives. It demonstrates how Freastar builds practical tools that solve real-world organizational challenges beyond meal ordering.

Key Features

Digital Pledge Page Creation

Easy-to-use tools for creating professional pledge pages that schools and communities can share publicly.

Parent & Community Participation

Simple participation tracking that allows parents and community members to sign pledges and show their support.

Phone-Free & Smartphone Agreements

Particularly useful for creating pledge pages around phone-free initiatives and smartphone usage agreements in schools.

Simple Public Sharing

Clean, shareable URLs that make it easy to promote pledge initiatives across social media and school communications.

School-Friendly Design

Clean, professional design that fits well with school websites and official school communications.

Easy Setup & Management

Straightforward setup process that doesn't require technical expertise, perfect for busy school administrators and parent coordinators.

Perfect for Community Initiatives

Phone-Free Pledges

Create pledges for phone-free school environments or specific phone-free times and areas.

Smartphone Agreements

Manage community agreements around responsible smartphone usage for children and teens.

Community Values

Gather community support for school values, behavioral expectations, and shared commitments.

Environmental Initiatives

Coordinate pledges around environmental responsibility and sustainability practices.

Health & Wellness

Create pledges supporting healthy lifestyle choices and wellness initiatives in school communities.

Safety Commitments

Organize community commitments around school safety, anti-bullying, and supportive environments.

How it works

1

Create your pledge

Set up your pledge page with clear descriptions of the initiative and what participants are committing to.

2

Share with your community

Use the shareable URL to promote the pledge through school communications, social media, and parent networks.

3

Track participation

Monitor pledge sign-ups and see how your community is responding to the initiative.

4

Build community momentum

Use the visible participation to build momentum and encourage more families to join the pledge.
