About LiveOnWith.me

LiveOnWith.me is a living collection of the moments, voices and photographs that made a loved one who they were. It is designed as a dignified alternative to social media — a quiet space made for remembering, not for scrolling.

A person’s life is not only the years they lived, but the stories that remain in everyone they touched. LiveOnWith.me gives families a permanent, private home for those stories.

Key Features

Curated storytelling

Share the milestones and cherished details of a life — the moments that shouldn’t be forgotten.

Shared photograph collection

Family and friends can contribute photographs. Every image is reviewed and approved by the family before it appears.

Community memory contributions

Friends, colleagues, neighbours and extended family can add memories, so the full picture of a life can be preserved.

Family moderation

Every contribution is moderated by the family before publishing. The family stays in control of their loved one’s story.

Cemetery search

Helps families and visitors locate existing memorials when searching for a loved one.

No public engagement metrics

No likes, no counts, no feeds. Made for remembering, gently and privately.
