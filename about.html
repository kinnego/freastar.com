About Freastar

Building practical digital platforms that solve real-world problems for schools, caterers and communities.

Our Story

Freastar grew from direct experience with the admin challenges that schools and catering companies face every day. Rather than building software in isolation, we started by understanding the real workflows around school meals, community engagement, and educational administration.

What we discovered was that many of the digital tools available to schools and caterers were either too complex, too generic, or didn't align with how these organizations actually operate. Too often, great intentions around technology ended up creating more work rather than less.

That's why Freastar focuses on practical software that fits naturally into existing workflows. Our platforms are designed around the real needs of parents ordering school meals, caterers managing daily preparation, schools tracking dietary requirements, and communities organizing around shared values.

Our Mission

Practical Solutions

We build software that makes daily administrative tasks easier, not harder. Every feature is designed around real workflows and genuine operational needs.

Supporting Local Providers

Our tools help local caterers compete with larger operators by giving them access to professional digital platforms that were previously out of reach.

School-First Thinking

Everything we build starts with understanding how schools actually operate, from meal ordering to community engagement and administrative reporting.

Community Focus

We believe in technology that strengthens communities rather than replacing human connections. Our platforms facilitate better communication and coordination.

Our Approach

Real-World Testing

Our platforms are built and tested in actual school and catering environments, ensuring they work under real operational pressures.

Iterative Improvement

We continuously refine our software based on feedback from the people who use it daily - parents, school administrators, and catering staff.

Technology That Scales

While our focus is on practical solutions, we build on modern, scalable technology that can grow with our users' needs.

Irish Context

Our software is designed with Irish schools, catering companies, and communities in mind, understanding local contexts and operational realities.

What Drives Us

Solving Real Problems

We're motivated by the opportunity to solve genuine administrative challenges that schools and caterers face every day, not by building technology for its own sake.

Empowering Local Businesses

We believe local caterers should have access to the same quality of digital tools as large operators, helping them serve their communities more effectively.

Transparent Operations

Our platforms are designed to make processes more transparent and accountable, whether that's allergen information, meal ordering, or community pledge tracking.

Work with us

If you're a school, catering company, or community organization looking for practical digital solutions, we'd love to hear about your challenges and see how we can help.
