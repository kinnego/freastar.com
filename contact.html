Common Questions

How long does implementation take?

Implementation timelines vary based on your needs. A basic Freastar Meals setup typically takes 2-4 weeks, while custom platforms may take longer. We'll provide a detailed timeline after understanding your requirements.

Do you work with schools outside Ireland?

While our primary focus is on Irish schools and caterers, we're open to discussing projects in other locations. Contact us to explore the possibilities.

Can you integrate with existing systems?

Yes, we can often integrate with existing school management systems, payment processors, and other platforms. Let us know about your current setup and we'll advise on integration options.

What kind of support do you provide?

We provide comprehensive support during setup and ongoing assistance for platform users. This includes training, technical support, and guidance on best practices.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Our approach is flexible and depends on your specific needs. We'll discuss terms that work for your organization during our initial conversation.

Can we see a demo first?

Absolutely! We're happy to provide demos of our platforms and discuss how they might work for your specific use case. This is often the best way to understand what we can offer.
