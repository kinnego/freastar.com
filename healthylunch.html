About HealthyLunch.ie

HealthyLunch.ie is one of the existing Freastar-powered school meal platforms, demonstrating how our technology works in real-world school lunch ordering scenarios. It provides a complete solution for healthy school lunch ordering with streamlined workflows for all users.

Key Features

Simple Parent Access

User-friendly ordering interface that busy parents can use quickly and easily, whether on mobile or desktop.

Child Profiles

Individual profiles for each child with dietary requirements, preferences, and allergen information clearly tracked.

Meal Selection

Easy meal selection process with clear options, nutritional information, and allergen details for informed choices.

School & Caterer Workflows

Streamlined backend processes for schools and catering companies to manage orders, preparation, and delivery efficiently.

Comprehensive Reporting

Detailed reporting tools that help schools and caterers track orders, dietary requirements, and operational metrics.

Installable Web App

Progressive web app that can be installed on phones, tablets, and computers for quick access and offline functionality.
